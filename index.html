What is AI (Artificial Intelligence)? AI refers to computer systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, making decisions, and solving complex problems. Modern AI leverages machine learning and deep learning to continuously improve from data and experience.

What is Agentic AI? Agentic AI represents the next evolution of artificial intelligence—autonomous systems that can act independently, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. Unlike traditional AI that simply responds to inputs, agentic AI can plan workflows, use tools, interact with APIs, and coordinate complex tasks with minimal human intervention.

In our webinars, we explore LLM workflows, intelligent agents, AI copilots, and practical automation patterns that are transforming how we build and deploy intelligent systems.
